# Demo video script: Yorisoi (target 2:42, hard limit under 2:50)

Recorded in Lens Studio's **Interactive Preview** (device: Spectacles), in the **Sunlit Room** preview scene. No SPECS device is used, and the end card says so.

The story is one working session with Yori: she waits quietly → you call her → briefing with live weather → rehearse a talk on cue cards only you can see → translate a word on the page → the stretch reminder fires → quick stretch → music to wind down → Voice only → a 10-second montage of everything else → end card.

**Two kinds of text on screen. Keep them apart:**

- **Her subtitles** are part of the Lens: a bubble beside her head with English (big) and Japanese (small). They show up on their own when she speaks.
- **Captions** are added in editing: English, burned in, bottom third, light text on a soft dark band. They tell the judges what is happening and what is live or sample. Never let a caption cover her bubble, a card label (live / offline / sample data / demo text) or the talk list.

**Preview controls:** click = pinch. Right-drag (or hold Shift) = look around. WASD = move, Q / E = down / up.

### 5. Rehearse on cue cards only I can see (0:42–1:20)

The longest shot. It carries hero feature 1. Cut the dead time; keep the talk list in frame the whole time.

- **0:42–0:50.** **Click:** **Mode** until the badge on her bubble reads "Cue cards mode" (cut the in-between presses). **Preview shows:** she steps back to her spot and the whole talk list for the "Yorisoi introduction" deck appears beside her. **Yori says:** "Ready when you are. I've got your cards." **Caption:** "Cue cards mode: my whole talk list, visible the entire time. Yori steps aside."

### 6. Real translation on the page (1:20–1:38)

## Recording and editing notes

**As recorded (2026-11-08):** the whole take is driven hands-free by the `demo-reel` LEAF scenario
(`Assets/Tests/DemoReelScenario.ts`). Head turns are done with the real preview camera (so the Sunlit
Room turns too); the reel waits for each turn, and the waits are cut. The Lens Studio window was
captured silently with Windows Graphics Capture (ffmpeg `gfxcapture`), and the sound was rebuilt from
the Lens Studio log: every `DeskCompanion says [id]` line is placed with its real voice MP3, plus the
original music, ducked under her voice. Shot 6 is a pick-up take looking 10° left so the word card
stays in frame. Captions sit in the left margin beside the preview, so they never cover her bubble or
the cards. Later additions: a My song pick-up after the music shot, and a 17-second developer voice-over
over the quiet opening ("An AI partner on a screen always feels a little fake… Meet Yori.", AI
text-to-speech). Final cut: 2:48.
